# Demo asset sources

The active replacement carbine is authored in `Weapons/Carbine.blend` by
`Weapons/build_carbine.py`. Its FBX exports are imported with
`game/shooter/demo/Scripts/import_carbine_v2.py`. The older procedural Kestrel
assets are retained as legacy content.
The WAV files here are deterministic synthesized project audio, generated by
`prepare_first_person.py` (legacy) and `create_training_audio.py` (active layered
training cues). They do not contain recordings or samples from Apex Legends,
Titanfall, or Epic sample projects.

The active arms derive from Epic's template `SKM_Manny_Simple`, retaining its
original textures, skeleton, skinning and rifle animations under `Content/Characters`. They are
selected from the installed UE 5.8 Templates and Feature Packs, with their hard
dependencies; the separate First Person Sample project is not used. These
assets remain Epic Unreal Engine content subject to its original terms.
`Content/demo/Character/SK_ViewmodelArms` contains only arm geometry. Its material
derivatives under `ViewmodelMaterials` disable the template's masked camera
proximity fade; the source mannequin, materials and textures are preserved.

The legacy arms mesh under `Content/demo/Character/Mannequin` derives from Epic's
bundled `MoverTests/Characters/Mannequins/Meshes/SKM_Manny_Simple`. The skeleton
and skinning are retained; body/head/leg geometry is removed. This remains Epic
Unreal Engine content, subject to its original terms. No First Person Sample
project was imported or used. Runtime dependencies on Mover content are removed.
